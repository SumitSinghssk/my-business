We design and build iOS and Android apps that feel native, work offline, and are straightforward to maintain, usually from a single shared codebase so you reach both platforms without paying for two teams.

Cross-Platform by Default

For most products we build with Flutter or React Native, sharing business logic and UI across iOS and Android while still using native features such as the camera, biometrics, location and notifications. Where a product needs deep platform work, we write native Swift or Kotlin modules.

Offline-First and Fast

Mobile users lose signal in lifts, warehouses and trains. We store data locally, sync changes in the background and resolve conflicts safely, so the app keeps working without a connection and never loses what the user entered.

What's Included

  • Product design for both platforms, following Apple and Google guidelines.
  • Secure authentication, including biometric login.
  • Push notifications and deep links into the right screen.
  • Analytics and crash reporting from the first build.
  • App Store and Google Play submission and release management.

The Backend Behind the App

Most apps need an API, an admin panel and somewhere to store data. We build those too, designed alongside the app so the whole product ships together and scales together.

After Launch

We plan for the operating system updates, device changes and store policy updates that arrive every year, and offer ongoing support so your app stays fast, secure and compatible.
