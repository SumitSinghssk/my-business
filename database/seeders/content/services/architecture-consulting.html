Technical Audits

Architecture and Roadmap Planning

Choosing between a rewrite and incremental improvement, splitting a monolith, adopting a new platform: we help you weigh the trade-offs with evidence and produce a roadmap that balances new features with technical health.

Fractional CTO and Mentoring

For growing companies without a senior technical leader, we provide part-time technical leadership: hiring support, architecture decisions, vendor evaluation and mentoring your engineers as the team grows.
