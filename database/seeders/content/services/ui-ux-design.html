Good design makes complex products feel simple. Our research-led UI/UX practice helps teams understand their users, design intuitive products and build design systems that keep every screen consistent as the product grows.

Research First

We start by understanding the people who will use your product: interviews, analytics review, usability testing of what exists today and journey mapping. Decisions are grounded in evidence rather than opinion.

From Wireframes to Prototypes

  1. Information architecture: structure, navigation and content hierarchy.
  2. Wireframes: layouts for every key flow, reviewed early and cheaply.
  3. Visual design: high-fidelity screens that reflect your brand.
  4. Prototypes: clickable flows tested with real users before development.

Design Systems

A design system gives designers and engineers one shared language. We create design tokens, a component library in Figma and matching coded components, with documentation that explains when and how to use each one. New features get faster to design and build, and the product stays consistent.

Accessibility

We design to WCAG 2.2 AA: sufficient colour contrast, keyboard navigation, clear focus states, meaningful labels and support for screen readers. Accessible products are better products for everyone and reduce legal risk.

Working with Your Engineers

Our designers work alongside engineers throughout delivery, not just at handoff. Designs are checked against real data and technical constraints early, which avoids costly rework later.
