When off-the-shelf tools no longer fit how your business works, we build custom software that automates your processes, connects your systems and removes the spreadsheets and manual steps that slow your team down.

Problems We Solve

  • Manual processes that rely on copying data between tools.
  • Disconnected systems with no single source of truth.
  • Legacy software that is expensive to run and risky to change.
  • Reporting that takes days to compile by hand.

Workflow Automation

We map how work actually flows through your organisation, then build software around it: approvals, notifications, scheduled jobs and exception handling. The result is fewer errors, faster turnaround and a clear record of who did what and when.

Integrations and APIs

We connect ERP, CRM, finance and industry-specific systems through REST and GraphQL APIs and event-driven integrations, so data is entered once and flows everywhere it is needed.

Modernising Legacy Systems

Replacing a critical system in one step is risky. We modernise incrementally, running old and new side by side, migrating data carefully and switching over module by module, so the business keeps running throughout.

“The best migration is the one your customers never notice.”

Built to Be Owned

You receive the full source code, documentation, automated tests and deployment pipelines, so your own team or any future partner can maintain and extend the system with confidence.
