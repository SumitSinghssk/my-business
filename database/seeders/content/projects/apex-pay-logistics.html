Apex runs cross-border freight with a fleet of more than 6,000 vehicles. They needed a driver app that handles routing and payments reliably, even where mobile coverage drops out.

The Challenge

Drivers were paid through a slow manual process, and route changes were sent by phone call. Existing apps failed in border areas with poor signal, which meant lost delivery records and disputed payments.

Our Approach

We designed the product offline-first. Every action a driver takes is saved on the device and synced when a connection returns, with conflict rules agreed with Apex's finance team up front.

  • A Flutter app for iOS and Android from a single codebase.
  • Live vehicle telemetry through AWS IoT.
  • A PostgreSQL ledger for settlements with full reconciliation.

What We Built

Drivers receive routes, capture proof of delivery and see their earnings update in real time. Settlements are calculated automatically once a delivery is confirmed, and the operations team manages everything from a web dashboard.

The Outcome

Driver payments that used to take days now settle the same day, delivery records are complete even on remote routes, and support calls about missing payments fell dramatically.
