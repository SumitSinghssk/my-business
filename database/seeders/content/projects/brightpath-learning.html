Brightpath Learning delivers online professional courses to thousands of learners. Their off-the-shelf learning platform could no longer support the way they teach, certify and report on progress.

The Challenge

Course teams worked around the old platform with spreadsheets and manual certificate emails. Corporate clients wanted progress reports for their staff, which took the operations team days to prepare every month.

Our Approach

We interviewed learners, instructors and corporate clients, then designed a platform around three needs: engaging lessons, automatic certification and self-service reporting.

  • A Laravel and Vue web application with a flexible course builder.
  • Automatic certificates issued when learners pass assessments.
  • Company dashboards with live progress and exportable reports.

What We Built

Instructors build courses from video, reading and quiz blocks. Learners pick up where they left off on any device, and corporate managers track their team's progress without contacting support.

The Outcome

Monthly reporting that took days is now instant, certificate requests no longer need manual handling, and course completion rates have improved.
