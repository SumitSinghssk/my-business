Summit Stays operates boutique hotels and holiday homes in mountain destinations. They wanted guests to book directly and manage their stay from one app instead of relying on third-party booking sites.

The Challenge

Most bookings came through online travel agencies, costing Summit Stays significant commission. Guests had no simple way to check in, request services or contact staff before and during their stay.

Our Approach

We mapped the complete guest journey, from first search to checkout, and focused on the moments where a direct relationship adds the most value: booking, arrival and in-stay requests.

  • A React Native app for iOS and Android with direct booking and secure payments.
  • Mobile check-in and digital room keys.
  • Integration with the property management system for live availability.

What We Built

Guests book rooms, pay, check in and message reception from the app. Staff see requests in real time and can offer upgrades and local experiences to guests before they arrive.

The Outcome

Direct bookings grew significantly in the first season, commission costs fell, and guest satisfaction scores rose thanks to faster check-in and easier communication.
