GenoSync supports oncology research teams with genomic sequencing analysis. They needed a secure platform that brings sequencing results and live patient biomarkers together for clinicians and researchers.

The Challenge

Analysis pipelines ran as scripts on individual workstations, results were shared as spreadsheets, and there was no reliable audit trail. As study volumes grew, turnaround times stretched and compliance reviews became difficult.

Our Approach

We worked with GenoSync's scientists to turn their proven analysis steps into a repeatable, monitored pipeline, and designed the clinician-facing interface around the questions they ask most often.

  • Python and PyTorch models packaged as versioned pipeline stages.
  • A FastAPI service layer with role-based access to patient data.
  • Kubernetes workloads that scale with sequencing volume.

What We Built

Samples move through ingestion, analysis and review automatically, with every step logged. Clinicians see results and biomarker trends in a clear React interface, and researchers can compare cohorts without exporting data.

The Outcome

Analysis turnaround is now measured in hours instead of days, results are reproducible, and a complete audit trail makes compliance reviews straightforward.
