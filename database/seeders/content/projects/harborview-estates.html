Harborview Estates is a growing property agency with offices in four cities. Their old website was slow, hard to update and generated very few enquiries from organic search.

The Challenge

Listings were entered twice, once in the agency's CRM and again on the website, so the site was often out of date. Pages loaded slowly on mobile, and property pages were not structured for search engines.

Our Approach

We rebuilt the website around the listings data the agency already had, so every property is published automatically from the CRM, and designed the search experience for people browsing on their phones.

  • A Laravel CMS synced with the agency's CRM every few minutes.
  • Fast property search with filters, maps and saved searches.
  • Structured data for every listing and neighbourhood page.

What We Built

Buyers and renters can search by area, price and features, book viewings and receive alerts for new properties. Agents manage neighbourhood guides and team pages themselves, without developer help.

The Outcome

The site now loads in under two seconds on mobile, listings are always current, and organic enquiries have grown steadily since launch.
